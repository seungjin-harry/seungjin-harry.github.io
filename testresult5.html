E41

Three prices for one plan

S1CP-PRICE-004 (new)

TestCompare the price of the Basic plan across the pricing page, the price API, the dry-run estimate, billing plans and both invoices.

SourcePrice for Basic (1 vCPU, 2 GiB)
Pricing page /pricing/ and price API$11.64 / month
deploy --dry-run estimate$11.64 / month
cpctl billing plans$14.55 / month = $11.64 × 1.25
November invoice (paid 6 Nov, Processing)$14.55 + Sales tax (Gyeonggi-do) 8% $1.16 = $15.71
October invoiceLines $11.64 + tax $0.93 = $12.57, total shown $14.55. Gap $1.98 = $11.64 × 0.25 − $0.93

ResultThe billed unit price is exactly 25% above the published price, and that factor explains October's $1.98 gap. The tax line reads "Sales tax (Gyeonggi-do) 8%", while Korea applies 10% VAT.
