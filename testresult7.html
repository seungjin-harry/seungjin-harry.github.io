x402 Mainnet Readiness Plan

Compute Portal plans to switch x402 USDC payments on Base from testnet to mainnet. On-chain settlement cannot be reversed, and Coinbase offers no SLA or service credits for its facilitator. This plan lists what to test on Base Sepolia first, the gates for going live in order, and the decisions the team needs to make. It sets no dates: each gate opens when the one before it has passed.

1Summary

Biggest risk
Paid, but no service

A customer's USDC is settled on-chain, then provisioning fails. It cannot be charged back, so only our own refund can fix it.

No safety net
No SLA, no credits

If the Coinbase facilitator is down, payments stop. A backup facilitator is our own decision.

Facts to settle
Fee and gas differ

The meeting notes and Coinbase's public docs disagree on the per-payment fee and on who pays gas.

Good news
Mainnet is self-serve

KYB is done. Going live is a key and a network setting, so we go live when the gates are passed.

The plan in one line. Prove on Base Sepolia that we never settle a payment before the service is ready, that no payment can be used twice, and that every settled payment matches an order. Then take a small internal mainnet payment and one refund before opening to customers.

3Payment flow and where it can fail

The order of steps is the main protection. Settling last means a failure before settlement costs the customer nothing.

#StepWhoIf it fails hereFailure point
1Agent calls a paid route without payment; server answers 402 with the requirements (amount, USDC asset, network, payTo, timeout)Agent → PortalWrong amount or address in the quoteP
2Agent signs a USDC transfer authorization and repeats the call with itAgentInsufficient USDC, expired or replayed signatureR
3Portal asks the facilitator to verify (no money moves)Portal → CoinbaseFacilitator down: reply 503, provision nothingF
4Portal prepares the service: account, entitlement, resources readyPortalProvisioning fails: do not settle, nothing chargedO
5Portal asks the facilitator to settle (USDC moves, irreversible)Portal → CoinbaseSettle fails after step 4: hold the service, retry, or release itO
6Portal returns 200 with the settlement receipt (transaction hash)Portal → AgentResponse lost: the agent retries and must not pay twiceR
7Daily reconciliation: every settled transaction ↔ order ↔ service ↔ invoicePortalUnmatched payment: refund within the agreed timeM

Failure-point letters match the test groups below: P protocol contract, R replay and idempotency, F facilitator outage, O orphan settlement, M money reconciliation.

5Gates

Gates run in order. Each one passes on its criteria, not on a date.

G0

Ready to test

Paid routes listed; step order written down; failure switches built; decisions in section 6 made or owned.

G1

Testnet pass

All S1 and S2 cases in P, O, R, F, M, A pass on Base Sepolia; reconciliation shows zero unmatched.

G2

Mainnet canary

C1–C4 pass with internal wallets only; one real refund completed.

G3

Open to customers

Alerts on, runbook shared, llms.txt and docs updated, team sign-off.

Why gates, not dates: going live is self-serve, so waiting for a gate to pass costs little. Launching with an untested orphan or refund path can cost customer money that cannot be pulled back. Each gate should be a real go/no-go.
